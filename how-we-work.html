How we work

Slowly, honestly, and in a set order. This page shows what we follow, so you know what to expect.

Change is a skill. You can get better at it.

Most people aren't built for quick pivots, and that's normal. Handling change well can be learned.

You likely have what you need. What's often missing is time to think, a few good questions, and someone in your corner who tells you the truth with care.

We work slowly on purpose. No hurry, no checked boxes, and no bright lights. The answers you find here are yours, and they tend to stay.

The pasqueflower

It blooms in the Colorado foothills in early spring, often through late snow, before conditions look ready. You won't feel ready either. Start anyway.

What we believe

  • Your strongest years can still be ahead of you.
  • Leaving something you gave everything to takes more nerve than staying.
  • You always have a choice. Some of them are hard.

Discover, then bloom

Our name is the order we work in. First you get honest about where you are. Then you build the habits to keep going.

Discover

With Dr. Kim, in workshops and intensives.

  1. Face It

    What happened, and what patterns do you see?

  2. Name It

    What does it mean, and is it true?

  3. Live It

    What will you do differently now?

Live It is where a workshop ends and coaching begins. A few hours can show you the first move. Making it stick takes longer.
