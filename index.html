Coaching for the stretch between what ended and what's next

We're Dr. Kim Bland and Yvonne Padilla, two coaches in Colorado Springs. We work with men and women through the changes nobody trains you for: leaving the military, changing careers, retiring, and the quiet house after the kids move out. Kim helps you see where you are. Yvonne helps you keep moving.

Who we work with

Smart, capable, and stuck.

Maybe you just took off the uniform after twenty years, and civilian work feels like a foreign language. Maybe the kids are gone, the job feels empty, and you've started asking yourself, quietly, whether this is it. Maybe you walked away from something you gave everything to and have no idea what comes next.

You're probably the one everyone else calls for advice. Right now you can't find your own footing, and you're tired of pretending you're fine.

We work with men and women, in separate groups and one-on-one.

If any of these fit, let's talk

  • Career change or job loss
  • Leaving the military for civilian work
  • Retirement or leaving a long-held role
  • Empty nest or shifting family roles
  • Coming home after years of being away for work
  • Divorce or the end of a relationship
  • Losing your sense of identity, purpose, or direction
  • Knowing something has to change, without knowing what

One thing worth knowing: this is coaching, not therapy. If something from the past comes up, we'll acknowledge it, then bring you back to what you're deciding now. If what you're carrying needs more than coaching, we'll say so and help you find the right person.

Discover, then bloom

Our name is the order we work in. First you get honest about where you are. Then you build the habits to keep going.

After the workshop

Some people leave with what they came for. Others want to keep going. Every person needs something different, so we build the plan together.

  • Root Session

    45 minutes with Yvonne

    Most people who feel stuck already know something has to change. What they can't see is what's holding them in place. In a Root Session, we find it. You leave knowing what's in the way and your first move.

  • One-on-one coaching

    Regular sessions with Yvonne

    BLOOM, at your pace. Most people need about three months; some need longer, and we'll decide together.

  • Intensives

    With Dr. Kim, by conversation

    Face It, Name It, Live It, in one focused stretch, for one person or a small group.
